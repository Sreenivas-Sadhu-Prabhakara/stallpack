    Settings

    The symbol changes nothing in the maths — all amounts are stored and computed in integer minor units (paise/cents). ₹ uses Indian digit grouping (₹1,23,456.75); $, € and £ use 1,000-grouping.

    Your data

    Everything is stored in this browser's localStorage under the key stallpack.v1. Nothing is ever uploaded — the page's security policy blocks all network requests. CSV export is the only way data leaves, and you trigger it.

    Honest limits

    • The reconciliation is only as good as your two physical counts — a miscount at either end shows up as variance, and stallpack cannot tell which count was wrong.
    • Variance is pure arithmetic (actual takings − expected at list price). Discounts, haggling, freebies, breakage, miscounts and theft are indistinguishable in it. This is not theft detection, and the verdict never accuses anyone.
    • Expected cash assumes list prices. If you routinely haggle, expected will overstate and variance will run negative — that is a feature to see, not an error.
    • All data lives in this browser's localStorage only. Clearing site data or switching devices loses history — export CSV regularly (the app reminds you every 5 saved days).
    • Pack suggestions are “your best day sold N at this market” — a simple history lookup, not demand forecasting.
